Camps are grouped by Mon–Fri week. Tap a week to filter, tap any card for full details, the heart to save, and inside a camp pick which days your child is going to share with friends. Holiday starts Thu 9 Jul; the first full camp week is 13 Jul.

About verification & safety. Every camp here was checked on the provider's own website. Where a price, time or exact week wasn't clearly stated we show Check website and link the booking page — please confirm before paying. Germany has no single camp inspectorate like England's Ofsted; most providers are registered clubs/charities (e.V.) or established schools — always confirm current details, ratios and safeguarding with the provider.
